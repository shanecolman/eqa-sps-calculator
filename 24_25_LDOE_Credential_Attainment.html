Executive Summary & Strategic Takeaways
Key findings across EQA's 2025 Post-DataCert Credential Attainment file

1. The NET Leads Network Rate & Mean Points

The NET Charter HS posted a 50.0% graduation rate (36/72) and 53.1 mean points, outperforming NOA (48.3%, 49.0 pts) and The NET 2 (46.7%, 48.0 pts excl. 1 blank LAA 1 record).

2. Retained Attendees Are the #1 Opportunity

Of the 109 zero-point students, 82 (75.2%) are classified as "Attendee" rather than Dropout—meaning they stayed connected to EQA and represent immediate 5th-year / credit-recovery wins.

3. SPED & Homeless Subgroups Outperform Peers

Special Education students graduated at 58.7% (59.2 mean pts) vs. 45.7% for non-SPED. Homeless students graduated at 55.2% (56.4 mean pts) vs. 46.0% for non-homeless peers.

4. Gender Gap & Low IBC Bonus Penetration

Male students lag female peers by 10.9 percentage points in graduation (42.4% vs. 53.3%) and 11.6 mean points. Only 9 of 106 graduates (8.5%) earned 110 or 150 credential bonus points.
